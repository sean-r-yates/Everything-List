FAQ

How do I add an item?

Use the plus button at the bottom right, paste a link, then edit anything the site could not read.

What does Export do?

It downloads the selected profile and list section as a CSV file you can open in Excel, Sheets, or a text editor.

Can I recover deleted items?

Open Bin from the sidebar. Deleted items can be restored until old Bin cleanup clears their full details.

Why are some prices or images missing?

Some sites block scraping or hide details behind scripts. Open the item editor to fill those fields manually.
